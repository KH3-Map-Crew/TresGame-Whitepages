## UModel

Let's you view cooked models/materials/animations and then extract them for view in 3D modeling programs. Additionally, you can extract all of the files from the compressed paks into cooked uassets so that you may view them using UAssetGUI.


## UAssetGUI
Additionally, download uassetGUI. This let's you parse cooked files and change variables. You have to extract your files using umodel or another program.
