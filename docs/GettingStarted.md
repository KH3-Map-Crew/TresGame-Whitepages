## UModel

Let's you view cooked models/materials/animations and then extract them for view in 3D modeling programs. Additionally, you can extract all of the files from the compressed paks into cooked uassets so that you may view them using UAssetGUI. There is a KH3 specific version of uModel available in the open kh discord linked below (discord app should take you straight to the resource, but the app might not automatically pop up).

[uModel](https://discord.com/channels/409140906625728532/828067806284611615/972658025351479296)


## UAssetGUI
Additionally, download uassetGUI. This let's you parse cooked files and change variables. You have to extract your files using umodel or another program.

[UAssetGUI](https://github.com/atenfyr/UAssetGUI)
